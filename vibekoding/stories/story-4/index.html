04 Lao Huang's Rules for Building from Zero04 Lao Huang's Rules for Building from Zero

After a long journey of trial, error, and persistence, Lao Huang summed up several lessons he paid for the hard way:After a long journey of trial, error, and persistence, Lao Huang summed up several lessons he paid for the hard way:

  • The building-block rule: do not try to swallow everything at once. Change one small feature at a time, and move on only after that part works.The building-block rule: do not try to swallow everything at once. Change one small feature at a time, and move on only after that part works.
  • Learn to give examples: when talking to AI, do not stay abstract. Show it concrete examples, error messages, and the effect you want.Learn to give examples: when talking to AI, do not stay abstract. Show it concrete examples, error messages, and the effect you want.
  • Learn by borrowing: do not just copy and paste. Try to understand why AI wrote it that way.Learn by borrowing: do not just copy and paste. Try to understand why AI wrote it that way.
  • Adjust your mindset: do not panic when errors appear. They are teaching you where the pitfalls are.Adjust your mindset: do not panic when errors appear. They are teaching you where the pitfalls are.
