[1. Two Cycles of Divergence and Convergence](#top-dd)[1. Two Cycles of Divergence and Convergence](#top-dd)

The Double Diamond is a classic design process framework promoted by the UK Design Council. It represents a full design and innovation process as two connected diamond shapes.The Double Diamond is a classic design process framework promoted by the UK Design Council. It represents a full design and innovation process as two connected diamond shapes.

It is called a "diamond" because each diamond contains two opposite but equally important motions:It is called a "diamond" because each diamond contains two opposite but equally important motions:

  • diverge: open the view and look at more possibilitiesdiverge: open the view and look at more possibilities
  • converge: narrow the scope and make choicesconverge: narrow the scope and make choices

The full process has four steps:The full process has four steps:

  1. Discover: broadly understand users, problems, context, and marketDiscover: broadly understand users, problems, context, and market
  2. Define: extract the core problem that is actually worth solvingDefine: extract the core problem that is actually worth solving
  3. Develop: explore multiple solution directions around that problemDevelop: explore multiple solution directions around that problem
  4. Deliver: choose, prototype, test, and deliver the more suitable solutionDeliver: choose, prototype, test, and deliver the more suitable solution

    5. The first two stages address the problem space; the last two address the solution space.The first two stages address the problem space; the last two address the solution space.
