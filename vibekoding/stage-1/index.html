Dari Nol ke Prototipe Pertama 🚀 Zero to Your First Prototype 🚀

⏱️ ~15 jam santai~15 relaxed hours 🧒 Level: Anak SMP bisa!Level: Middle-school friendly! 🎯 Output: 1 prototipe nyataOutput: 1 real prototype

Selamat datang di Level 1! Di sini kamu nggak butuh pengalaman coding sama sekali. Kita mulai dari memahami apa itu vibe coding, mencoba bikin program pertamamu lewat obrolan dengan AI, sampai membangun prototipe produk sungguhan. Santai aja — kalau bingung, itu wajar. Kalau ketawa, itu bonus. 😄 Welcome to Level 1! You don't need any coding experience here. We start by understanding what vibe coding is, building your first program by chatting with AI, all the way to creating a real product prototype. Take it easy — confusion is normal. Laughter is a bonus. 😄

Kurikulum Level 1Level 1 Curriculum

6 pelajaran menuju produk pertamamu 6 lessons to your first product

LESSON 01 · ~45 min

🧭 Apa Itu Vibe Coding?🧭 What Is Vibe Coding?

Peta petualanganmu. Kenapa ngobrol sama AI = masa depan programming, dan gimana cara belajar di kursus ini. Your adventure map. Why chatting with AI = the future of programming, and how to learn in this course.

 LESSON 02 · ~2 hours

🐍 Bisa Ngomong = Bisa Coding🐍 If You Can Talk, You Can Code

Bikin game ular AI-native pertamamu — langsung di browser, tanpa install apa pun. Yes, kamu baca benar. Build your first AI-native snake game — right in the browser, no installs. Yes, you read that right.

 LESSON 03 · ~2 hours

🛠️ Kenalan sama AI IDE🛠️ Meet Your AI IDE

Pindah dari browser ke "markas" developer sungguhan: IDE bertenaga AI. Belajar cara ngobrol yang efektif dengan AI. Move from the browser to a real developer's "headquarters": an AI-powered IDE. Learn to chat with AI effectively.

 LESSON 04 · ~3 hours

💡 Berburu Ide Produk💡 Hunting for Product Ideas

Ide bagus nggak jatuh dari langit — tapi bisa "ditangkap". Belajar nemu masalah nyata yang layak dijadikan produk. Great ideas don't fall from the sky — but they can be "caught". Learn to find real problems worth turning into products.

 LESSON 05 · ~4 hours

🏗️ Bangun Prototipe Pertamamu🏗️ Build Your First Prototype

Ubah satu ide jadi aplikasi web sungguhan: dari analisis kebutuhan sampai halaman-halaman yang bisa diklik. Turn one idea into a real web app: from needs analysis to clickable pages.

 LESSON 06 · ~3 hours

✨ Masukkan Kekuatan AI + Pamerkan!✨ Add AI Powers + Show It Off!

Tambahkan fitur AI ke produkmu, lalu publish ke internet biar bisa dipakai orang lain. Momen paling memuaskan! 🎉 Add AI features to your product, then publish it to the internet for others to use. The most satisfying moment! 🎉
🐢 Saran Belajar dari KamiOur Study Advice

Selesai lebih penting daripada sempurna. Kerjakan pelajaran satu per satu, praktikkan langsung (jangan cuma dibaca!), dan jangan takut salah. Error itu bukan kegagalan — error itu AI yang lagi ngajak kamu ngobrol lebih lanjut. 😜 Kalau kamu anak SMP: ajak temanmu belajar bareng, siapa tahu kalian bikin startup beneran. 🚀 Done is more important than perfect. Go lesson by lesson, practice hands-on (don't just read!), and don't fear mistakes. Errors aren't failures — errors are AI inviting you to chat some more. 😜 If you're in middle school: invite a friend to learn together — who knows, you might build a real startup. 🚀
