😂 Jokes PembukaOpening Joke

"Kok nggak langsung ngoding aja?" 🤨 Karena bikin produk tanpa tahu masalahnya itu kayak bikin payung buat ikan: capek bikinnya, nggak ada yang pakai. 🐟☂️ Ide bagus itu bukan jatuh dari langit — tapi bisa diburu. Dan tebak apa senjata terbaiknya? Keluhan orang-orang di sekitarmu. Keluhan = emas. Mulai sekarang, tiap denger orang ngeluh, matamu harus berbinar. ✨ "Why not just start coding?" 🤨 Because building a product without knowing the problem is like making umbrellas for fish: exhausting to build, nobody uses it. 🐟☂️ Great ideas don't fall from the sky — they can be hunted. And guess the best weapon? Complaints from people around you. Complaints = gold. From now on, whenever you hear someone complain, your eyes should light up. ✨

1. Kenapa Mulai dari Masalah, Bukan dari Kode? 🎯1. Why Start from Problems, Not Code? 🎯

Banyak kursus coding nyuruh kamu bikin Todo List, kalkulator, blog pribadi. Boleh aja buat latihan — tapi masalahnya: arah salah, makin dalam makin nyasar. Bayangin kamu capek-capek 2 minggu bikin "aplikasi kalender" padahal udah ada 100 yang lebih bagus. 💀 Di dunia nyata, produk yang berhasil selalu mulai dari rasa sakit seseorang (pain point). Gojek lahir karena susahnya dapat ojek. Tokopedia lahir karena ribetnya belanja online zaman dulu. 🛵🛒 Many coding courses tell you to build a Todo List, a calculator, a personal blog. Fine for practice — but here's the problem: wrong direction, the deeper you go the more lost you get. Imagine grinding for 2 weeks on a "calendar app" when 100 better ones already exist. 💀 In the real world, successful products always start from someone's pain (pain point). Gojek was born because getting a motorcycle taxi was hard. Tokopedia was born because online shopping used to be a hassle. 🛵🛒

⚖️ Saringan Ide Juara (4 Pertanyaan Ajaib)The Champion Idea Filter (4 Magic Questions)

Sebelum jatuh cinta sama sebuah ide, tanya 4 hal ini: Before falling in love with an idea, ask these 4 things:

  1. Seberapa sering masalahnya muncul? (Tiap hari? Seminggu sekali?) 📅How often does the problem happen? (Daily? Weekly?) 📅
  2. Seberapa sakit rasanya? (Kesel dikit vs bikin nangis?) 😭How painful is it? (Mildly annoying vs makes you cry?) 😭
  3. Gimana orang menyelesaikannya sekarang? (Kalau mereka udah rela keluar waktu/uang, itu tanda bagus!) 💸How do people solve it today? (If they already spend time/money on it, that's a great sign!) 💸
  4. Siapa penggunanya? Bisa sebutin orangnya secara spesifik? 👤Who's the user? Can you name a specific person? 👤

2. Contoh Kisah: Si Ming & Uang Kas Kelas 💰2. A Story: Ming & the Class Treasury 💰

Di kursus aslinya ada karakter bernama Xiao Ming yang mengumpulkan banyak masalah dari keseharian. Kita bikin versi lokalnya: Si Ming, bendahara kelas 1 SMP yang tiap Jumat pusing. 😵 The original course has a character named Xiao Ming who collects many everyday problems. Let's make a local version: Ming, a 7th-grade class treasurer who gets a headache every Friday. 😵

Keluhan yang dia dengar:
• "Duit kas kurang terus, dicatat di mana sih?" 🧾
• "Aku udah bayar kok dicatat belum!" 😤
• "Catetannya ilang gara-gara buku PR-nya ketumpahan es teh." 🧋💀 Complaints he hears:
• "The treasury is always short — where is it even recorded?" 🧾
• "I already paid but it's not written down!" 😤
• "The notebook got lost because my homework book got drenched in iced tea." 🧋💀

Sekarang kita saring pakai 4 pertanyaan ajaib: Now let's filter it with the 4 magic questions:

Di pelajaran asli, proses ini disebut validasi kebutuhan: ubah "kayaknya bagus nih" jadi "ternyata emang dibutuhkan". Total: 17/20 — this idea is WORTH HUNTING! 🎯 Not because it sounds cool, but because there's real evidence. In the original course this is called needs validation: turning "seems nice" into "actually needed".

3. 6 Langkah Perburuan 🏹3. The 6-Step Hunt 🏹

1️⃣📏

Pasang KriteriaSet Criteria

Siapkan 4 pertanyaan ajaib di atas sebagai saringan. Tulis di notes biar nggak lupa. Prepare the 4 magic questions above as your filter. Write them down so you don't forget.

2️⃣👂

Kumpulkan KeluhanCollect Complaints

Dari misi Pelajaran 1: catatan 5 masalahmu + ngobrol sama 3 orang (keluarga/teman/guru). Tanya: "Apa sih yang paling bikin ribet belakangan ini?" From Lesson 1's mission: your list of 5 problems + chat with 3 people (family/friends/teachers). Ask: "What's been the biggest hassle lately?"

3️⃣👥

KelompokkanGroup Them

Pisahkan per "siapa yang sakit": siswa? ibu rumah tangga? pedagang kantin? Penjual online? Masalah yang sama sering muncul di kelompok yang sama. Sort by "who's in pain": students? parents? canteen sellers? online sellers? Similar problems often cluster in the same group.

4️⃣🎬

Bayangkan AdegannyaPicture the Scene

Untuk tiap masalah, tulis: siapa, kapan, di mana, apa yang terjadi, dan kenapa itu nyebelin. Makin konkret, makin bagus. "Ibu lupa belanjaan" → "Ibu di pasar, Jumat sore, HP penuh foto daftar belanja yang nggak bisa dicari." For each problem, write: who, when, where, what happens, and why it's annoying. The more concrete the better. "Mom forgets groceries" → "Mom at the market, Friday afternoon, phone full of shopping-list photos she can't find."

5️⃣🧪

Validasi!Validate!

Jalankan 3 ide terbaik lewat 4 pertanyaan ajaib. Buang yang skornya jelek tanpa sedih — ide itu kayak kue: yang gosong dibuang, bukan ditangisi. 🍪💔➡️🍪😄 Run your best 3 ideas through the 4 magic questions. Drop the low scorers without sadness — ideas are like cookies: the burnt ones get tossed, not mourned. 🍪💔➡️🍪😄

6️⃣📝

Tulis Konsep ProdukWrite the Product Concept

Untuk pemenang, tulis 1 kalimat sakti: "Untuk [siapa], yang bermasalah dengan [apa], produk ini membantu [bagaimana]." For the winner, write one magic sentence: "For [who], who struggles with [what], this product helps [how]."

4. Misi Rahasiamu 🕵️4. Your Secret Mission 🕵️

🎯 MISI LEVEL 1-D: Perburuan BesarMISSION LEVEL 1-D: The Great Hunt
  1. Kumpulkan minimal 8 masalah (5 dari catatanmu + 3 dari ngobrol sama orang lain). 🎣Collect at least 8 problems (5 from your notes + 3 from talking to others). 🎣
  2. Saring semuanya dengan 4 pertanyaan ajaib. Tulis skornya. 📊Filter everything with the 4 magic questions. Write down the scores. 📊
  3. Tulis 3 kalimat konsep produk untuk 3 pemenang. 🏆Write 3 product concept sentences for the 3 winners. 🏆
  4. Tanya AI pakai prompt bonus di atas, bandingkan hasilnya dengan pilihanmu. Kalau beda, pikirkan kenapa. 🤔Ask AI using the bonus prompt above, compare its picks with yours. If they differ, think about why. 🤔

5. Uji Pemahaman 🧪5. Knowledge Check 🧪

❓ Kenapa produk harus mulai dari masalah, bukan dari ide keren?❓ Why must products start from problems, not cool ideas?▶
Karena produk yang nggak menyelesaikan masalah nyata nggak akan dipakai orang, sekeren apa pun teknologinya. Masalah = alasan orang mau pakai (bahkan mau bayar). Ide tanpa masalah = payung buat ikan. 🐟☂️ Because products that don't solve real problems won't get used, no matter how cool the tech is. Problems = the reason people will use (even pay for) it. Ideas without problems = umbrellas for fish. 🐟☂️
❓ Sebutkan 4 pertanyaan ajaib untuk menyaring ide!❓ Name the 4 magic questions for filtering ideas!▶
1) Seberapa sering masalahnya muncul? 2) Seberapa sakit dampaknya? 3) Gimana orang menyelesaikannya sekarang? 4) Siapa penggunanya secara spesifik? 📅😭💸👤 1) How often does it happen? 2) How painful is it? 3) How do people solve it today? 4) Who exactly are the users? 📅😭💸👤
❓ Apa itu kalimat konsep produk?❓ What's a product concept sentence?▶
Satu kalimat yang merangkum produkmu: "Untuk [siapa], yang bermasalah dengan [apa], produk ini membantu [bagaimana]." Contoh: "Untuk bendahara kelas yang sering salah catat uang kas, aplikasi KasKu membantu mencatat pemasukan & pengeluaran otomatis di HP." 💰📱 One sentence summarizing your product: "For [who], who struggles with [what], this product helps [how]." Example: "For class treasurers who keep mis-recording treasury money, the KasKu app helps log income & expenses automatically on the phone." 💰📱
