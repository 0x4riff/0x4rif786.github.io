The move from page to program usually begins with four capabilities:The move from page to program usually begins with four capabilities:

  1. Real data: users can save, edit, and delete information instead of looking at hard-coded examples.Real data: users can save, edit, and delete information instead of looking at hard-coded examples.
  2. A backend: accounts, business rules, and secret keys do not live in the browser.A backend: accounts, business rules, and secret keys do not live in the browser.
  3. Failure handling: empty input, lost connections, and failed requests explain what happened and what to do next.Failure handling: empty input, lost connections, and failed requests explain what happened and what to do next.
  4. A real release: other people can open, install, or update the product without connecting to your computer.A real release: other people can open, install, or update the product without connecting to your computer.

    Adding a chat box does not automatically make a product AI-native.Adding a chat box does not automatically make a product AI-native.

    Traditional software waits for the user to click through a fixed sequence. An AI-native product first understands the outcome the user wants, reads the required context, uses controlled tools, and asks for confirmation before consequential actions.Traditional software waits for the user to click through a fixed sequence. An AI-native product first understands the outcome the user wants, reads the required context, uses controlled tools, and asks for confirmation before consequential actions.

    For example, a normal meeting tool asks users to copy tasks by hand. An AI-native meeting assistant can:For example, a normal meeting tool asks users to copy tasks by hand. An AI-native meeting assistant can:

    • ask who owns an unclear task or when it is due;ask who owns an unclear task or when it is due;
    • prepare task drafts in the team's task system;prepare task drafts in the team's task system;
    • show the drafts for approval before sending them.show the drafts for approval before sending them.

    The important design decision is the division of work:The important design decision is the division of work:

    • people own goals, high-risk decisions, and final approval.people own goals, high-risk decisions, and final approval.
