3.3 Password Strength Check3.3 Password Strength Check

CODE 
^(?=.*[a-z])(?=.*[A-Z])(?=.*\d).{8,}$

Breakdown:Breakdown:

  • (?=.*[a-z]) — at least one lowercase letter (lookahead assertion)(?=.*[a-z]) — at least one lowercase letter (lookahead assertion)
  • (?=.*[A-Z]) — at least one uppercase letter(?=.*[A-Z]) — at least one uppercase letter
  • (?=.*\d) — at least one digit(?=.*\d) — at least one digit
  • .{8,} — total length at least 8 characters.{8,} — total length at least 8 characters

5. Greedy vs. Lazy: A Key Difference5. Greedy vs. Lazy: A Key Difference

CODE 
Text: <b>hello</b> and <b>world</b>
PatternMatch ResultExplanation
.*hello and worldGreedy: match as much as possible
.*?helloLazy: match as little as possible
💡 Tips Praktis💡 Pro Tip

The default is greedy mode. Add ? after a quantifier to switch to lazy mode. Most of the time, you want lazy mode.The default is greedy mode. Add ? after a quantifier to switch to lazy mode. Most of the time, you want lazy mode.
