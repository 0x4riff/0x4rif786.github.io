Di Level 1 kamu bikin makanan di dapur minjem. Di Level 2, kamu belajar buka restoran beneran: dapur (backend), ruang makan (frontend), gudang bahan makanan (database), kurir (deployment), sampai kasir (pembayaran 💳). Modul-modul di bawah ini adalah kurikulum lengkap dari Easy-Vibe yang sudah kami kurasi — tiap modul punya versi Inggris resminya, dan ringkasan humornya di sini. 😄 In Level 1 you cooked in a borrowed kitchen. In Level 2, you learn to open a real restaurant: the kitchen (backend), the dining room (frontend), the pantry (database), the delivery crew (deployment), and even the cashier (payments 💳). The modules below are the complete curated Easy-Vibe curriculum — each module links to its official English version, with our humorous summaries here. 😄

🗺️ Saran Urutan Belajar Level 2Suggested Level 2 Study Order

Jalur tercepat ke produk nyata: Backend 1 (Supabase) → Backend 2 (API) → Project 1 (SaaS) → Backend 4 (Deploy) → Backend 6 (Pembayaran). Frontend bisa dipelajari sambil jalan. Jangan perfeksionis: yang penting produknya hidup dulu, baru dirapikan. 💪 The fastest path to a real product: Backend 1 (Supabase) → Backend 2 (API) → Project 1 (SaaS) → Backend 4 (Deploy) → Backend 6 (Payments). Frontend can be learned along the way. Don't be a perfectionist: get the product alive first, then polish it. 💪
