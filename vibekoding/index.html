Cara BelajarnyaHow It Works

4 langkah. Sesimpel masak mie instan. 🍜 4 steps. As simple as cooking instant noodles. 🍜

1️⃣👀

Nemu MasalahFind a Problem

Lihat sekeliling. Apa yang bikin kamu atau orang lain sebel? Itu peluang produk! Look around. What annoys you or others? That's a product opportunity!

2️⃣🗣️

Ngobrol sama AIChat with AI

Jelaskan idemu ke AI pakai bahasa sehari-hari. Makin jelas ngomongnya, makin bagus hasilnya. Describe your idea to AI in everyday language. The clearer you speak, the better the result.

3️⃣🛠️

Revisi & PolesRevise & Polish

Hasil pertama jarang sempurna — dan itu normal. Bilang ke AI apa yang perlu diubah, ulang sampai puas. First results are rarely perfect — that's normal. Tell AI what to fix, repeat until you're happy.

4️⃣🚀

Pamerkan ke DuniaShow It to the World

Deploy biar bisa diakses orang lain. Minta feedback. Perbaiki lagi. Selamat, kamu Product Engineer! 🎉 Deploy so others can use it. Ask for feedback. Improve again. Congrats, you're a Product Engineer! 🎉
